Individual Defense

The 'SLCD' Personal Defense Protocol

A simple, memorable 4-step mental firewall that community members can apply whenever confronted with urgent digital demands.

1. S - Stop

Whenever a message conveys immense urgency ('A/C blocked in 2 hours', 'Arrest warrant issued', 'Electricity cut tonight'), intentionally pause. Urgency is designed to hijack your amygdala and bypass logical skepticism.

✔ Take a deep breath; no legitimate authority requires a 5-minute deadline.

2. L - Look

Inspect sender credentials closely. Check the actual email domain address (e.g., does it say @sbi.co.in or @sbi-support-portal.net?). In SMS, verify whether the sender code is a legitimate 6-character header or a standard 10-digit phone number.

✔ Scrutinize URLs for hidden characters, bit.ly shorteners, or spelling errors.

3. C - Call & Check

Never call the phone number provided inside the suspicious SMS or email. Instead, independently look up the official contact number from the back of your debit card or the official portal, and call them directly to verify.

✔ Out-of-band verification destroys 99% of social engineering attacks.

4. D - Decide & Defend

If anything feels suspicious, immediately block the number, report the message as spam/phishing, and never enter OTPs, passwords, or scan unknown QR codes. If compromised, contact 1930 instantly.

✔ Remember: Entering a UPI PIN always deducts funds, never receives money.
Organizational & Merchant Security

Customer Data Protection: Best Practices & DPDP Act

Community businesses, coaching centers, clinics, and local startups frequently handle sensitive customer data. Protecting this data is both an ethical responsibility and a legal mandate under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

01

Data Minimization

Collect only the customer information that is strictly required to fulfill the service. Do not demand Aadhaar or PAN copies unless required by banking regulation.

  • No Unnecessary KYC: Avoid collecting identity cards for routine retail sales.
  • Purpose Limitation: Use customer numbers only for billing, not promo calls.
  • Timely Deletion: Purge customer logs and inquiry files after active service concludes.
02

Storage & Access Control

Prevent internal leaks and unauthorized exposure by replacing unencrypted spreadsheets with access-controlled, encrypted databases.

  • No Plaintext Spreadsheets: Never leave customer lists in public cloud folders.
  • Role-Based Access (RBAC): Only authorized staff should access customer records.
  • Mandatory 2FA: Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication for all business CRM and email logins.
03

Compliance & Breach Plan

Align with India's DPDP Act obligations and maintain an immediate incident response plan if customer data is compromised.

  • Explicit Consent: Provide transparent notice explaining why data is collected.
  • Breach Notification: Notify the Data Protection Board and users promptly.
  • Staff Awareness Drills: Train retail staff against social engineering and pretext callers.

Incident Protocol

What to Do If You or a Community Member Get Scammed

Time is the single most decisive factor in recovering stolen funds. Follow this emergency response ladder immediately.

Step 1: Golden Hour Call

Dial 1930 immediately. The Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System can freeze recipient accounts in real-time across participating banks before the cash is laundered.

Call 1930 Now
Step 2: Bank Block

Log into your mobile banking app or call your bank's toll-free emergency number to immediately freeze your netbanking, block debit/credit cards, and reset UPI PINs.

Step 3: Lodge Online FIR

File an official complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Upload transaction reference numbers, screenshots of chats, fake websites, and phone numbers.

Open Portal
Step 4: Device Cleanup

If you installed any remote software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, APK files), uninstall them immediately, run a malware scan, and perform a factory reset if necessary.
